# GestPro — store listing (English)

**Title (30):** GestPro – Rental Management

**Short description (80):** Rents, receipts, arrears and buildings under control. For landlords & agencies.

**Full description:**
GestPro is the all-in-one application for landlords, property managers, real-estate agencies and residences. Manage your buildings, units, tenants and leases; invoice every rent automatically; record payments (cash, mobile money, bank transfer); issue numbered receipts that cannot be altered; follow arrears and send reminders; handle deposits, maintenance, utilities and expenses — all from your phone or computer, in English or French.

Why GestPro
• Automatic monthly invoices and rent schedules
• Payments and immutable receipts, reversals with approval
• Arrears at a glance, reminder notices, late fees, payment plans
• Interactive 3D view of each building with the rooms of every unit
• Maintenance work orders, technicians and vendors
• Concierge: visitors, parcels, incidents, keys
• Owner view, tenant portal, accountant and cashier roles — 13 roles with fine permissions
• Reports and CSV exports: occupancy, collections, arrears ageing, revenue, expenses
• Two-factor authentication, account lockout, complete audit trail
• Built for Cameroon and Africa: FCFA, Douala time, mobile money
• Secure cloud hosting with daily backups

GestPro is provided to companies after approval by Bouquet Innovation. Request your access code in the app and start managing your properties within minutes.
