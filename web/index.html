Set up your Redmi in 3 steps

MIUI needs USB debugging turned on so the computer can talk to the phone.

  1. 1

    Unlock Developer options

    Settings → About phone → tap MIUI version seven times.

  2. 2

    Enable USB debugging

    Settings → Additional settings → Developer options → turn on USB debugging and USB debugging (Security settings).

  3. 3

    Authorize the computer

    Plug in, then tap Allow on the “Allow USB debugging?” prompt on the phone.

Locked out of your own phone?

Honest help for the “I forgot my own PIN” case.

Wouapit-Androman does not bypass or crack lock screens. There is no feature here that defeats someone else’s pattern, PIN or password, and none that unlocks a phone you don’t own. Tools that claim to do that mostly exist to open stolen devices, and we won’t build that. If you’re locked out of a device that is yours, the safe, supported routes below all prove ownership and erase the screen lock by resetting the phone.
🔎

Find Device (remote erase)

Sign in to your Xiaomi account at i.mi.com or your Google account at google.com/android/find, select the device and choose Erase. Removes the lock using credentials only you have.

♻️

Factory reset (Recovery)

Power off, hold Volume Up + Power to enter recovery, then Wipe data / factory reset. Erases everything and clears the lock. You’ll re-verify your account on first boot.

🔓

Mi Unlock (bootloader)

Xiaomi’s official Mi Unlock tool unlocks the bootloader after signing in to the Mi account bound to the phone and a mandatory waiting period. For advanced, owner-authorized reflashing.

All three require that you can sign in to the account on the device, and all erase data — that’s what keeps your phone (and everyone else’s) safe from unauthorized access.
